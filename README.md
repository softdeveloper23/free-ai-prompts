# Free AI Prompts by softDev23

29 copy-and-paste AI prompts for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any AI chat assistant. Most have fill-in [blanks] for your own details. They cover checking an AI answer, research with real sources, studying, writing, planning and job hunting.

**Web version, with fill-in fields and a copy button:** https://softdev23.com/free-ai-prompts/

## How to use

1. Pick a prompt below.
2. Replace each [bracketed blank], if it has any, with your own details. Leave out anything private you don't need to share.
3. Paste it into your AI chat.

Many prompts ask the AI to mark guesses, use only the facts you give it, and link real sources. AI can still be wrong, so check anything important.
