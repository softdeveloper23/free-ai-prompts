# Plan a trip on a budget

**Group:** Plan and get unstuck · **Kits:** none · **For:** Everyone, Students

Replace any [bracketed blanks] with your own details, then paste the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another AI chat.

```text
Help me plan a trip.

Where and when: [e.g. Lisbon, four days in May]
Who's going: [e.g. two adults, one child aged 8]
Total budget: [amount and currency, and what it should cover]
Travel style: [e.g. slow mornings, lots of walking, love food markets]
Must-dos or must-avoids: [anything]

Ask me up to two questions if something important is missing. Then give me a simple day-by-day plan with one or two main things per day, rest time, and rough cost ranges. Show a budget table with what each part might cost. Say which prices, opening times or rules I must check myself because they change, and don't present guesses as current facts. Suggest one cheaper swap for each day in case money gets tight.
```
