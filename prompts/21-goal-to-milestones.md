# Break a big goal into milestones

**Group:** Plan and get unstuck · **Kits:** Side project · **For:** Builders, Creators, Small business

Replace any [bracketed blanks] with your own details, then paste the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another AI chat.

```text
Break this big goal into milestones I can actually finish.

Goal: [e.g. launch my first app, or run a half marathon]
Deadline: [a date, or "no deadline"]
Time I can give it: [hours per week]
What's already done: [anything so far]

Give me three to six milestones in order. For each: what "done" looks like in one sentence, a rough time estimate and what it depends on. Mark the riskiest milestone and suggest how to test it early. Then give me one task I can do today in under an hour. If the deadline doesn't fit the time I have, say so plainly and suggest what to cut.
```
