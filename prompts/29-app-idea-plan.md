# Turn an app idea into a simple plan

**Group:** Create and build · **Kits:** Side project · **For:** Builders

Replace any [bracketed blanks] with your own details, then paste the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another AI chat.

```text
Turn my app idea into a simple plan I can build.

The idea: [describe it in a few sentences]
Who it's for: [the people who'd use it]
Platform and my skills: [e.g. iPhone, and I'm learning Swift]

Ask me up to three questions if the idea is unclear. Then write:
1. The problem in one sentence, from the user's point of view.
2. The smallest first version: three to five must-have features, and what to leave out for now.
3. The main screens, one line each.
4. The biggest risks (technical, and whether people want it) and a cheap way to test each.
5. A first week of build tasks.

Keep it realistic for one person. Don't invent market numbers or competitors; tell me what to research instead.
```
