# Plan my week realistically

**Group:** Plan and get unstuck · **Kits:** none · **For:** Everyone, Students, Freelancers

Replace any [bracketed blanks] with your own details, then paste the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another AI chat.

```text
Help me plan a realistic week.

Things I need or want to do: [list them, with deadlines if any]
Fixed commitments: [work hours, classes, appointments]
My energy: [e.g. best in the morning, tired after 8 pm]

Don't fill every hour. First, tell me honestly if this is too much for one week, and what could move or be dropped. Then give me a simple day-by-day plan with the most important task first each day, some buffer time and breaks. Put tasks that need focus in my best-energy hours. Finish with the three tasks that matter most this week, so I know what to protect if things go wrong.
```
