# Plan a YouTube video with a strong start

**Group:** Create and build · **Kits:** Creator · **For:** Creators

Replace any [bracketed blanks] with your own details, then paste the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another AI chat.

```text
Help me plan a YouTube video.

Topic: [e.g. how I built my first iPhone app]
Who's watching: [e.g. beginners who want to start coding]
Length: [e.g. eight minutes]
What viewers should leave with: [the one useful thing]

Give me:
1. Three openings (the first 15 seconds) that show the payoff early without overpromising.
2. A simple outline with rough timestamps, where the useful part starts early and each section leads into the next.
3. Three honest title ideas and a thumbnail text idea of three to five words.
4. One natural spot to mention my link or product, after the viewer has already got something useful.

Don't invent facts, results or stories about me. Ask if you need them.
```
