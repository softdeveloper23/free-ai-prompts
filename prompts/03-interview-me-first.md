# Ask me questions before you answer

**Group:** Keep your AI work going · **Kits:** none · **For:** Everyone

Replace any [bracketed blanks] with your own details, then paste the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another AI chat.

```text
I want help with: [your task, e.g. a birthday party plan, a cover letter or a workout routine]

Before you answer, interview me. Ask one question at a time, up to five questions, starting with whichever of these seems least clear from what I've told you: my goal, my limits, or who or what this is for. Keep each question short, and give an example answer if it might be hard to answer. After my last answer, sum up what you learned in a few bullet points, then give me the result. If I say "skip", move on, make a sensible assumption and tell me what you assumed.
```
