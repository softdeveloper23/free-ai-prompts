# Build a realistic study plan

**Group:** Learn and remember · **Kits:** Study · **For:** Students, Builders

Replace any [bracketed blanks] with your own details, then paste the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another AI chat.

```text
Build me a realistic study plan.

Topic or skill: [e.g. basic Spanish, Excel formulas or Swift]
Where I'm starting: [what I already know]
Time I have: [e.g. 30 minutes a day, four days a week]
Goal or deadline: [e.g. hold a five-minute conversation by December]

Ask me up to two questions if something important is missing. Then give me a week-by-week plan with what to learn, how to practice it (by doing, not just reading) and a small check at the end of each week to see if it stuck. Make the first week easy to start. Suggest types of free resources, but don't invent specific links or book titles. If my goal isn't realistic for the time I have, say so and suggest a smaller one.
```
