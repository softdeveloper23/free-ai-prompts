# Brainstorm ideas, then pick the best

**Group:** Create and build · **Kits:** Creator · **For:** Creators, Small business, Builders

Replace any [bracketed blanks] with your own details, then paste the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another AI chat.

```text
Brainstorm ideas for [what you need ideas for], then help me pick.

Who it's for: [the audience]
My limits: [budget, time, skills, anything to avoid]

Give me 15 ideas that are really different from each other: some safe, some unusual, a few bold. One line each. Skip the obvious ones most people would think of first. Then score your top five against my limits in a short table (effort, cost and fit for the audience). Recommend three, explain why in one sentence each and give the first small step for each. Don't claim any idea will succeed; say what I'd need to test.
```
