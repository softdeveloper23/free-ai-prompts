# Help me decide when I'm torn

**Group:** Research and decide · **Kits:** none · **For:** Everyone

Replace any [bracketed blanks] with your own details, then paste the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another AI chat.

```text
Help me think through a decision I'm stuck on. Don't decide for me.

The decision: [e.g. take the new job or stay]
The options: [list them]
What matters most to me: [e.g. money, time with family, learning]

Ask me up to three short questions, all in one message, that would clarify what I actually want. After I answer:
1. Show how each option does on what matters to me, in a simple table.
2. Tell me which parts are easy to undo and which aren't.
3. Point out any assumption I seem to be making that I should check.
4. Ask me: "A year from now, which choice would you be more disappointed you didn't try?"

Keep it balanced, and tell me what information would change the picture.
```
