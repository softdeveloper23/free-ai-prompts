# Write a clear email

**Group:** Write and reply · **Kits:** Job hunt · **For:** Everyone, Freelancers, Small business

Replace any [bracketed blanks] with your own details, then paste the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another AI chat.

```text
Help me write an email.

Who it's to, and how well I know them: [e.g. my landlord, we've spoken twice]
What I want to happen after they read it: [e.g. fix the heater this week]
Key facts to include: [dates, numbers, what already happened]
Tone: [friendly, firm or formal]

Use only the facts I gave you. If something the reader would need is missing (like a date, amount or deadline), ask me up to two questions first. Then write:
1. Three subject lines.
2. A short version (under 120 words) with one clear request.
3. A slightly longer version, in case the situation needs more context.

Use plain words and skip filler like "I hope this email finds you well." Mark anything you had to guess with (check this).
```
